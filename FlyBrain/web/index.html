Flappy Bird played by a real fruit fly brain

97,973 neurons and 1.5 million synapses of the FlyWire 783 adult Drosophila connectome, simulated as leaky integrate-and-fire cells at 1 ms resolution.
The world (what the fly sees)
Rendered from the same luminance field that is fed to the 10,582 photoreceptors, so this is literally the fly's view.
The fly's brain (frontal projection)
Every dot is one real neuron at its real position. Bright dots fired in the last few milliseconds; the two optic lobes sit on the outside, the descending neurons run down the middle.
