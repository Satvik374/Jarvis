# CapCut Desktop — Genre Playbooks

Pick the one matching the request. Each gives: **structure → pacing → must-use
tools → avoid**. All of them ride on the pipeline in `SKILL.md` §3 and the
recipes in `references/features.md`.

---

## Talking-head / Vlog (horizontal or vertical)
- **Structure:** Hook (best line first, ≤3s) → intro of promise → body in
  chapters → payoff/CTA.
- **Pacing:** Tight. Remove every pause and filler; the speaker should "jump"
  between sentences (jump cuts are the native style here).
- **Must-use:** Ripple-trim dead air (`Ctrl+B` + `Delete`, or `Q`/`W`). **B-roll
  on the track above to cover jump cuts** and show-don't-tell. **J/L cuts** so
  audio flows across picture cuts. Slow **keyframe push-in** on key sentences.
  Auto-captions. Noise reduction + leveled dialogue + light ducked music bed.
- **Avoid:** Long static takes, flashy transitions between every cut, loud music
  fighting the voice.

## Cinematic / Travel / B-roll story
- **Structure:** Establishing wide → sequence of motivated shots building a place
  or mood → emotional peak → resolve. Often music-led, minimal or no dialogue.
- **Pacing:** Let shots breathe (2–5s) but cut on motion and on the music.
- **Must-use:** **24fps** feel. **Speed ramps** (Curve) on movement. Consistent
  **LUT/filter** across all shots + per-shot Adjustment to match. Beat-synced
  cuts. Subtle **keyframe pans/push-ins** to add life to static shots. Sound
  design (ambience + risers under swells).
- **Avoid:** Over-grading (crushed blacks/neon skin), shaky un-stabilized shots
  (use Stabilization), random speed changes not tied to the music.

## Gaming montage / Highlights
- **Structure:** Cold-open with the best clutch → escalating highlights → biggest
  moment saved for a drop → outro.
- **Pacing:** Fast, beat-driven. Cut on the music; punch in on kills.
- **Must-use:** Source **1080p/4K @ 60fps** for clean slow-mo. **Speed ramps**
  (slow the perfect shot, speed the travel). **Keyframe zoom-punch** on kills.
  **Chroma key / auto-cutout** for facecam overlay (corner, own track). Effects
  (shake/RGB split/glitch) **only on hits**. Beat-synced hard cuts + SFX
  (impacts/whooshes). Bold captions for callouts.
- **Avoid:** Effects on every clip, music that buries the game/hitmarker audio,
  slow-mo that isn't ramped.

## Tutorial / How-to / Educational
- **Structure:** Promise/outcome first ("by the end you'll…") → numbered steps →
  recap/CTA.
- **Pacing:** Clear over fast. Hold on important screens; speed-ramp boring
  waits.
- **Must-use:** Screen-recording on main track; **keyframe zoom-ins** to focus on
  the exact UI element being discussed. On-screen **step text/labels & arrows**.
  Auto-captions. Clean leveled narration + very low music bed (or none). **Freeze
  frame** to pause on a key state.
- **Avoid:** Distracting effects, music louder than narration, tiny unreadable
  text, jump cuts that skip a step the viewer needs.

## Ad / Product / Marketing / UGC
- **Structure:** Pattern-interrupt hook (problem or bold claim, ≤2s) → product in
  action / benefit → social proof → strong CTA. AIDA.
- **Pacing:** Punchy, retention-obsessed; a beat/visual change every ~1.5–2.5s.
- **Must-use:** Best 2 seconds first. Fast **beat-synced cuts**, **zoom-punch
  keyframes** on the product, clean bright grade, bold **kinetic captions**,
  **auto-cutout** to place product on branded backgrounds, SFX on reveals. End on
  a clear CTA card. Export **9:16 1080p 30fps** for feeds.
- **Avoid:** Slow intros/logos up front, weak CTA, cluttered frames, muddy audio.

## Music video / Performance
- **Structure:** Driven entirely by the track — verses vs. chorus get different
  cut density.
- **Pacing:** Cut on the beat; increase cut frequency into choruses/drops.
- **Must-use:** **Beat markers** as the cut grid. **Speed ramps** on the bar.
  Strong stylized **LUT**. Effects on drops. Multi-angle intercutting. Keep
  performer lip-sync frame-accurate (nudge with `←`/`→`).
- **Avoid:** Cuts that drift off-beat, over-busy effects that hide the artist.

## Podcast / Interview / Multi-cam
- **Structure:** Cold-open with the spiciest 15s → conversation with visual
  variety → outro.
- **Pacing:** Conversational; cut to whoever's speaking, trim tangents/dead air.
- **Must-use:** Two+ angles on stacked tracks — cut between them to hide trims
  and add energy. **Auto-captions** (essential for clip reach). **J/L cuts**.
  Noise reduction + normalize both mics to matched loudness. Pull vertical
  **clips** for social via **Auto reframe** + big captions.
- **Avoid:** One static wide for 20 minutes, mismatched mic levels, uncorrected
  captions.

## Social short / UGC (TikTok · Reels · Shorts) — default vertical
- **Structure:** Hook (≤2s, on-screen text + verbal) → fast value delivery →
  loop-back or CTA. Aim to make it re-watchable.
- **Pacing:** Very fast, every second earns attention. Zooms, quick cuts, captions
  always moving.
- **Must-use:** **9:16, set before importing.** **Auto-captions** with a bold
  animated style (kept above the bottom ~15%). **Zoom-punch keyframes**,
  **beat-synced cuts**, trending SFX, one consistent filter. Trim ruthlessly to
  under target length. Export **1080p 30fps**.
- **Avoid:** Slow starts, tiny captions, letterboxed 16:9 in a 9:16 frame,
  Pro-badged assets that add a watermark.

---

### If the genre isn't listed
Combine the closest playbooks and fall back to the universal quality bar in
`SKILL.md` §6: hook fast, every cut earns its place, hard cuts by default,
consistent color, intentional/leveled audio, readable text, deliberate ending.
